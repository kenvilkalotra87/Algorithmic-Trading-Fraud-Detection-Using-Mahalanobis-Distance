How the Project Works in Simple Terms

For a college viva presentation, summarize the workflow in these six simple points:

  • 1. Data Ingestion: The system loads simulated trade records across 5 assets (TECH, BANK, AUTO, ENERGY, PHARMA).
  • 2. Pattern Learning: It estimates the normal multivariate distribution by calculating the mean vector (μ) and covariance matrix (Σ) across Price, Quantity, Volume, and Price Change.
  • 3. Distance Measurement: It computes the Mahalanobis distance for each trade to see how far it diverges from normal multivariate trading behavior.
  • 4. Volume Spike Analysis: In parallel, it calculates the statistical volume Z-score to detect unilateral order surges.
  • 5. Dual Classification: Trades exceeding either threshold are flagged with transparent reasons (Mahalanobis Distance, Volume Spike, or Both).
  • 6. Visual Exploration: Analysts inspect flagged trades, 2D scatter plots, and download CSV reports.

Key Conceptual Questions & Answers

What is Anomaly Detection?

Anomaly detection is the identification of rare items, events, or observations that raise suspicions by differing significantly from the majority of the data.

What is Algorithmic Trading?

Algorithmic trading is the automated execution of financial orders using pre-programmed trading instructions accounting for variables such as time, price, and volume.

What is Mahalanobis Distance?

Mahalanobis distance measures the distance between a point and a multivariate distribution. Unlike Euclidean distance, it accounts for differences in variance and correlations between features:

DM(x) = √[ (x - μ)T Σ-1 (x - μ) ]

Why is Mahalanobis Distance Useful in Trading?

In financial markets, features like price and quantity are strongly correlated with notional volume. Euclidean distance treats all features independently, which produces spherical error contours. Mahalanobis distance creates elliptical decision boundaries aligned with real market correlations.

What is a Covariance Matrix?

The covariance matrix captures the joint variability and directional dependencies between all pairs of numerical features in the dataset.

Why are Multiple Features Considered Together?

A trade might have an ordinary price and an ordinary volume individually, but having a large price change on tiny volume indicates severe liquidity divergence that univariate checks miss.

What is a Volume Spike?

A volume spike occurs when an order exceeds the typical market volume distribution by multiple standard deviations:

Zv = (Volume - μvolume) / σvolume

How does the System Classify Potential Anomalies?

A trade is classified as a POTENTIAL ANOMALY if its Mahalanobis distance exceeds the configured threshold (τM > 3.0) OR its volume Z-score exceeds the threshold (τV > 3.0).

Ethical Distinction: Anomaly vs Fraud

An anomaly is a statistical outlier, not proof of fraud. In real markets, unusual trades can arise from legitimate portfolio rebalancing, breaking news events, or operational mistakes. The system identifies candidates for human review.
